Turn “can u do these?” into a booking.

The problem

Every booking starts with a DM that says almost nothing.

📷 can u do these? saturday how much for knotless sat?? me and my sister want braids [voice note] hi um so I wanted to ask about…
  • Independent stylists run their business from Instagram DMs, mostly at night.
  • Before anything is booked she has to ask what they want, explain her price and rules, and offer a time.
  • Generic AI replies get her prices and rules wrong, and don’t sound like her.

The product

Vague message in. Specific reply out.

  • The photo is read into tags from her list: knotless, bum length, size.
  • A service sheet fills itself. Every field shows its source; anything without one is cleared.
  • The price comes from her price list, never from the model.
  • The reply is drafted in her voice, following her house rules.

One tap

She fixes it once. The price and the reply follow.

  • The reader said medium. She taps smedium: £200 becomes £220 and the reply re-drafts.
  • Five seconds to undo. Fixes made together go as one.
  • Replies she hasn’t sent later in the thread re-draft too. If she already told the client £200, it offers a one-tap correction.
  • When the client picks a time, Confirm booking sends the day, time and total in one message.

It learns from her

The next client gets it right first time. In her voice.

  • Her fixes: the closest past fixes guide each new draft.
  • Her photos: a photo fix applies to photos that look like it, not to every photo.
  • Her voice: drafts she rewrites teach how she writes. Prices and details are masked, so only the style carries over.

Closest yes

Every no comes with the closest yes.

  • Small knotless to the waist, max £150: large instead of small, £150. Her length stays.
  • A wig install: a sew-in instead. Babylights over budget: highlights.
  • Worked out from her price list, changing what clients care about least first. One tap applies it as a fix.

Technical execution

Built so a stylist can trust what it sends.

  • Nothing invented: every field quotes the DM or a confident photo tag, or it’s cleared.
  • Her numbers: prices and the closest yes come from her price list in code.
  • Conversations: a settled field changes only when the new message says so.
  • One Mubit task per stylist: the instructions depend only on her profile; her fix rides in the message.
  • Clean tests: scoring runs send x-middleware-record: off, so they never become training data.
  • Private by design: clients see only their own thread; keys never reach a browser.
  • Abuse limits: per-client and per-room rate limits, image size checks, uploads checked by their actual file type.
  • Stack: TanStack Start on Cloudflare Workers, one Durable Object per stylist, Effect Schema.
